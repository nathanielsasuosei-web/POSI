# POSI — Drug Store Operations Suite

A responsive, local-first **drug store** POS and operations demo built with React and Vite. It keeps owner and cashier workspaces distinct and adds invoicing and printable receipts, sales and stock management with low-stock alerts, expenses, business reports, and staff attendance tracking.

## Run locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

To test the **production offline shell** locally, build and run Vite's preview server (service workers are intentionally disabled in the development server):

```bash
npm run build
npm run preview
```

Production deployments must use HTTPS (or localhost) for service workers.

## Workspaces and workflows

- **Sign-in (username & password):** everyone signs in with a username and password. **Only the owner creates accounts** — there is no self-registration. On a fresh install the starter logins are owner `owner` / `owner123`, Ama Boateng `ama` / `ama123`, and Kwame Owusu `kwame` / `kwame123`; staff records saved before passwords existed are given the same starter credentials on first load. Change them after signing in. Passwords are stored only as salted SHA-256 hashes in this browser, never in plain text.
- **Owner credential control:** the owner can change **their own password** (top-bar **Password** button, current password required, or Staff Tracking → Login on their own card), **change any employee's username**, and **reset any employee's password** without knowing the old one — Staff Tracking → **Login** on the employee's card, or when creating/editing the staff account. Usernames must be unique (at least 3 characters) and new passwords at least 6 characters. Employees can change only their own password (top-bar **Password**, current password required); they cannot change their username or anyone else's login.
- **Owner workspace:** dashboard with low-stock alerts, customer-lead and maintenance previews, point of sale, stock & item management, customers, sales & receipts, expenses, gift cards & money owed, staff tracking, business reports, messages, activity timeline, maintenance and settings. **Only the owner** can send messages to staff and create employee accounts. The owner sees **every employee's sales history** — via the Sales History seller filter (employees and the owner), or per staff member from Staff Tracking → Sales history — and can **correct or delete** any recorded sale, including their own, with a required reason and a full correction trail.
- **Activity timeline:** a searchable chronological feed combines sales, stock movements, expenses, staff updates, messages and maintenance. Filter by event type or date range and export the visible timeline to CSV.
- **Customer leads:** named-customer purchase history is turned into practical sales leads. See what each customer mostly buys (ranked by quantity), other favourite items, visit frequency, lifetime/period value and last purchase. Start a repeat sale directly from a lead, call follow-up opportunities, and export the visible insights to CSV.
- **Maintenance:** schedule equipment and facility service with due dates, vendors, recurring intervals, notes and estimated costs. Overdue and seven-day alerts appear on the dashboard and maintenance workspace. Completing a service records its actual cost and notes, can create a Maintenance expense, and can automatically schedule the next recurring visit.
- **Settings:** update the store name, address and contact details used throughout the workspace and on receipts; configure new-invoice prefixes, the optional POS discount, default reorder level for new items and receipt footer; change your password; and export a complete JSON snapshot of local shop data.
- **Cashier workspace:** point of sale, items, customers, **Today's Sales** (only the sales the cashier made **today** — past sales, other cashiers' sales and the sales calendar are hidden, and a note explains that history is owner-only), and messages (read-only — cashiers cannot send messages). Cashiers can also clock in and out from the top bar. **Only the owner** sees low-stock alerts, reorder levels, cost/price margins, stock value and the stock movement log; cashiers get a read-only item list with price and availability.
- **Sales & receipts:** payments are **Cash, MoMo and Gift card** (Check and Account are removed). Every confirmed POS payment records a paid invoice with line items, customer, seller, payment method, totals and invoice number. Each sale can be **printed as a receipt** — the receipt shows the shop address, **seller name**, customer, **date and time**, payment method and **total amount**, and printing outputs only the receipt (or invoice) on the page. Each row in sales history has a **Print receipt** button. Invoices and receipts can also be exported to CSV from sales history. Sales history of **all workers** (and the per-employee history in Staff Tracking) is **owner-only**.
- **Stock management (owner only):** product records carry SKU, barcode, supplier, cost/price, stock quantity, a reorder level and an expiry date. Owners can restock (stock in) or record adjustments for damage, loss or count corrections; every unit in or out is written to a stock movement log (sales, restocks, adjustments) with who did it and when. Checkout deducts stock automatically and logs each sale movement. Cost, profit margin and stock value are hidden from cashiers.
- **Expiry tracking:** each item can carry an expiry date. Items past their date are marked **Expired** (and cannot be added to a sale at the POS), items inside 90 days are marked **expiring soon** (`EXPIRY_SOON_DAYS`). The Stock & Items table has an **EXPIRY** column, expiry KPI chips (expired / expiring ≤90d / expired stock at cost), expiry filters (expired, expiring soon, no expiry set), and the dashboard has an **Expiry alerts** card. The totals row sums expired and soon-to-expire counts and values under each column.
- **Stock movement log totals (owner only):** the log's totals row gives a total under every column — PRODUCT (moves and distinct products), TYPE (sales / restocks / adjustments plus **Stock +N · Adj ±N · N edits**), QTY (in, out and net units), UNIT COST (summed and average), VALUE AT COST (net, with the in and out money split) and PROFIT (summed with margin and the revenue it came from).
- **Product movement summary (owner only):** under the item list, each product is broken down by movement **TYPE** — Sale, Restock in and Adjustment — with **QTY** (units and how many movements), **UNIT COST**, **VALUE AT COST** (unit cost × quantity, negative for stock leaving) and **PROFIT** (sales only). Every product then closes with a bold **Product total** line that sums its units, unit cost, value at cost and profit **and** adds the **Stock**, **± Adj** and **Edits** totals for that product (units and number of clicks). The totals row sums each of those columns across every visible product — including the Stock / ± Adj / Edit grand totals — and the table follows the search, category, stock and expiry filters. **Only with moves** hides products with no movement history and the card can be hidden.
- **Stock movement log (owner only):** every unit in or out is valued at the item's **cost price** — the log shows UNIT COST, VALUE AT COST (cost × quantity, negative for out-movements) and **PROFIT** for sale movements, plus a totals row that sums units in/out/net, cost value moved, products sold and total profit with its margin. New movements store the cost price and selling price at the time they happen, and the full log can be expanded beyond the latest 15 rows.
- **Low stock alerts (owner only):** items at or below their reorder level raise alerts — a badge on the Stock & Items menu, a bell dropdown in the top bar with one-click restock, a dashboard alerts card, a low/out-of-stock filter in inventory, and a warning after any sale that pushes an item to its reorder level.
- **Expenses:** record business spending (rent, utilities, supplies, transport, salaries, maintenance, marketing) with date, payment method and notes. Entries can be edited, deleted, filtered by date range or category, and exported to CSV, with a spending-by-category breakdown.
- **Correcting and deleting sales (owner only):** every row in Sales History (and every sale on a staff member's history in Staff Tracking) has a **Correct** button. The owner can fix a mis-recorded sale — seller, customer, date, time, payment method, gift card, discount, line items, quantities and unit prices — and must type a **reason**. Saving recalculates the invoice subtotal, discount, total, units and profit, reconciles inventory (extra or returned units are written to the stock movement log as corrections), updates the customer's spend and visit count, adjusts gift-card balances, keeps the invoice number, and stores a **correction trail** (before → after, who and why) that shows on the invoice, the receipt and as a “✎ corrected ×N” badge in the sales tables. The activity timeline logs each correction. The owner can also **delete** a sale — stock is restored, the sale's movement entries are removed, customer spend and gift-card balances are reversed, and the deletion is logged. Employees never see corrections or the underlying history: their **Today's Sales** page only ever holds the sales they made today, and they cannot open, print or export any other sale.
- **Sales calendar (owner):** Sales History is owner-only and now opens with a month **sales calendar** — each day shows its revenue and sale count, today is outlined, days with sales are shaded. Click one day to filter it, click a second to select a date range (click the same day twice for a single day), navigate months, or use the Today / Yesterday / Last 7 days / This month / All dates presets. The table, KPI chips, totals row and CSV export all follow the selected range (and still respect the employee, payment and search filters), the month header summarises revenue, sales, units and profit, and the calendar can be hidden.
- **Products bought & profit:** sales history carries a **PROFIT** column (owner only, price − cost per line, summed per sale) and totals for **units bought, revenue, cost and summed profit with margin**. The dashboard shows **profit on all sales** with total products bought, business reports show units bought alongside revenue/COGS/gross profit (and the CSV export has units, cost, profit and a TOTAL line), and the customer list totals include products bought.
- **Action totals (owner):** under the Stock / ± Adj / Edit buttons the inventory totals row now sums what each action did for the visible items — units added by **Stock** (with how many restocks), the net **± Adj** quantity (with how many adjustments) and the number of **Edits**. Each item also tracks its own edit count (shown as a small badge on the Edit button, with the last-edited date in the tooltip).
- **Totals under every table:** inventory, stock movements, customers, sales history, gift cards, money owed, expenses, attendance, per-employee sales and the invoice line items all end in a **Totals** row — counts, units, hours and money summed directly under their own column, always following the active search and filters.
- **Business reports:** revenue, cost of goods sold, gross profit, expenses and net profit for today/week/month/all, a seven-day revenue-vs-expenses chart, payment-method breakdown, expenses-by-category breakdown, top products, net position and full CSV export.
- **Staff tracking:** the owner creates staff accounts; clock in/clock out from the top bar with automatic hours calculation, an attendance log with status and export, per-staff performance (sales count, total to owner, items sold, hours worked), a per-employee sales history, and a monthly payroll summary.
- **Offline:** on the first production visit while online, the service worker saves the app files on the device. The top bar shows **Online · offline ready** once the POS can open without internet. Inventory, invoices, expenses, attendance, stock movements, maintenance plans and settings are stored in this browser, so the app can reopen and keep working offline on that device. If the app files cannot be saved (for example, a file is missing after a redeploy), a banner explains the problem. It offers **Try again** and **Reset offline files**. Reset clears only the saved app files and never touches sales, stock or other shop records. Avoid "clear site data" in browser settings, because that also deletes the locally stored shop records.

## Demo limitations and data

The username-and-password sign-in is local-first demonstration authentication: credentials are checked against salted SHA-256 hashes stored in this browser, with no server verification, lockout, or password recovery beyond an owner reset. Owner and cashier workspaces share demo shop data **only inside this browser on this device**. There is no backend, cross-device synchronization, cloud backup or cross-browser data sharing. Clearing browser data removes the locally stored records. Starter products, sales, staff, expense and stock-movement records are sample data. Barcode scanning, payment processing, tax/dispensing validation and real pharmacy integrations are not connected.

Before using this for a real pharmacy, move staff accounts and password verification to a secure server (e.g. bcrypt/argon2, lockout and audit controls), a shared database and backups, audit logs, validated payment and receipt integrations, and the applicable dispensing, privacy, safety and tax controls. Confirm current Ghanaian pharmacy requirements before handling prescription sales or customer data.
